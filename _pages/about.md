I am a Biological Oceanographer that quantifies planktonic distributions and interactions across trophic, spatial, and temporal scales. My published research spans ~6 orders of magnitude in organism size, from phytoplankton-grazing dynamics to macrozooplankton ecology. I have an interdisciplinary skillset that combines field observations, experimental rates, existing datasets, and big-data computational approaches. My primary research interests are to better understand gelatinous zooplankton biogeochemical roles and to integrate observations from different observational platforms (in situ imaging + acoustics, satellites, eDNA, and more) with rate-measurements to pursue novel insights into ocean functions.
